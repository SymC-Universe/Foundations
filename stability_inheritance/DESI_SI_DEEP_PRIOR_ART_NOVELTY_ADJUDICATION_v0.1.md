## Question

Does the literature already contain an operational framework equivalent to the current SI staged representation qualification:

1. scalar compression versus multicomponent response;
2. removal of the scalar/common direction;
3. test of residual vector composition;
4. additive native-plus-residual representation;
5. explicit cross-block relational/interaction representation;
6. feature-count-matched control that destroys sample-level cross-block pairing while preserving declared marginals/internal vector organization;
7. source-held-out validation;
8. prospectively frozen positive, redundant, pairing-nonessential, indeterminate, and refusal outcomes?

## Search result

A dedicated deep literature search plus full-text reads of the closest precedents found **no direct end-to-end equivalent** to the full staged sequence.

However, nearly every individual statistical ingredient has strong prior art. Therefore novelty cannot attach to any one ingredient.

## Closest collisions

### 1. Classifier two-sample testing is established

Lopez-Paz and Oquab, “Revisiting Classifier Two-Sample Tests” (arXiv:1610.06545), formalize held-out classification as a two-sample test.

**Collision:** binary discrimination between posterior/model families is not novel.

**Residual:** SI uses classification only as one diagnostic inside a staged representation-admission/refusal workflow.

### 2. Pairing-destroyed multimodal controls are established

Axelsen/Bak/Hansen, “Testing Multimodal Integration Hypotheses with Application to Schizophrenia Data,” DOI 10.1109/PRNI.2015.20, permute sample indices within label groups to destroy cross-modal sample correspondence while retaining group structure.

**Collision:** destroying sample-level pairing between blocks while preserving important marginals/group structure is not novel.

**Residual:** the SI test places the pairing control after scalar-removal and additive-block qualification and requires it to adjudicate a specific relational-added-value question.

### 3. Conditional-independence null construction is established

Sen et al., “Model-Powered Conditional Independence Test” (arXiv:1709.06138), and “Mimic and Classify” (arXiv:1806.09708), construct joint-versus-null classification problems in which target pairing/dependence is removed conditionally while relevant marginal/conditional structure is preserved or corrected.

Berrett et al., “The conditional permutation test for independence while controlling for confounders,” JRSS B 82 (2020), DOI 10.1111/rssb.12340, gives a formal conditional permutation framework.

**Collision:** a pairing-destroyed or conditional-dependence null is mature methodology.

**Residual:** the present DESI SI circular-shift control is a simpler task-specific control, not a new conditional-independence test.

### 4. Coupling-specific / synergistic information is established

Liang et al., “Quantifying & Modeling Multimodal Interactions: An Information Decomposition Framework” (arXiv:2302.12247), uses Partial Information Decomposition to separate redundancy, uniqueness, and synergy. Synergy is explicitly tied to information in the observed coupling that is lost under alternative distributions preserving declared marginals.

**Collision:** the idea that joint coupling contains information not present in marginal blocks is not novel.

**Residual:** SI currently operationalizes this through held-out task performance and pairing controls rather than claiming a new information-theoretic definition of synergy.

### 5. Complementary information beyond established cosmological summaries is established

Makinen et al., “Hybrid summary statistics: neural weak lensing inference beyond the power spectrum,” JCAP 2025(01) 095, DOI 10.1088/1475-7516/2025/01/095, explicitly optimizes learned summaries to add Fisher information beyond established power-spectrum summaries and requires seed-matched simulations to model cross-summary covariance correctly.

**Collision:** asking whether richer cosmological summaries contain information beyond an established compressed representation is not novel.

**Residual:** SI is not learning a better summary statistic; it is qualifying scalar loss, residual composition, and pairing-specific cross-block added value under explicit refusal outcomes.

### 6. Staged diagnostic frameworks with indeterminate outcomes are established

Steiner et al., “When Are Multimodal Predictions Biologically Supported? A Diagnostic Evaluation Framework” (arXiv:2605.31504, 2026) uses a staged diagnostic procedure, subspace decomposition, permutation-based nulls, held-out cohorts, scenario classification, and an indeterminate outcome when evidence is insufficient.

**Collision:** a staged multirepresentation diagnostic framework with explicit indeterminacy is not novel by itself.

**Important difference:** the paper does not implement the same scalar-loss -> residual-composition -> additive -> cross-block-interaction -> pairing-destroyed comparison sequence.

### 7. Local posterior pairing diagnostics are established

Linhart, Gramfort, and Rodrigues, “L-C2ST: Local Diagnostics for Posterior Approximations in Simulation-Based Inference” (arXiv:2306.03580), uses classifier diagnostics in which equal observation marginals force discrimination to depend on parameter-observation pairing/conditional structure.

**Collision:** classifier sensitivity to pairing-specific posterior structure is established.

## Current novelty classification

### Not novel

- classifier two-sample testing;
- held-out logistic discrimination;
- multitracer/vector information;
- scalar compression being lossy;
- residualization/projection of common components;
- feature interaction terms;
- pairing-destroyed/permutation controls;
- conditional-dependence testing;
- synergy/redundancy decomposition;
- cosmological summaries that add information beyond standard summaries;
- staged diagnostic frameworks;
- indeterminate/refusal outcomes considered individually.

### Residual candidate

No direct precedent was found for the **full prospective Stability Inheritance representation-qualification chain** in which:

1. a scalar compression is tested and allowed to fail;
2. residual vector composition must retain held-out information after scalar removal;
3. native coordinates and residual composition are tested additively;
4. explicit cross-block relational terms are added;
5. observed pairing must beat both additive structure and a pairing-destroyed matched control;
6. the result is interpreted through representation admission/refusal rather than physical mechanism or model preference;
7. the same framework requires native-model equivalence and no-added-value outcomes to remain valid closures.

This is best classified provisionally as:

- **NEW_INTEGRATION:** supported;
- **NEW_DISCRIMINATING_TEST / EVIDENCE LADDER:** plausible and currently the strongest method claim;
- **NEW_BOUNDARY / REFUSAL ARCHITECTURE:** plausible;
- **NEW STATISTICAL PRIMITIVE:** refused;
- **NEW INFORMATION-THEORETIC DEFINITION:** refused;
- **NEW COSMOLOGICAL MECHANISM:** refused;
- **FIELD-FIRST METHOD:** not yet authorized.

## Stronger native comparators identified by the literature

The deep search exposes two important comparators for future work:

1. **Partial Information Decomposition:** a formal comparator for whether apparent cross-block added value is redundancy, uniqueness, or synergy.
2. **Conditional permutation / conditional-independence tests:** a formal comparator for whether pairing-specific dependence remains after conditioning on declared native variables.

These should not be added to the active DESI v0.1d test. They are candidates for a separately frozen future validation stage after the current three-result adjudication.

## Manuscript framing consequence

A future SI methodology paper should not claim to invent pairing tests, residualization, C2ST, synergy, multiblock interaction, or indeterminate decisions.

The defensible methodological claim is instead that SI **assembles established statistical operations into a prospective stability-representation qualification ladder** designed to distinguish:

- scalar sufficiency;
- vector information beyond scalar compression;
- additive multiblock sufficiency;
- pairing-specific relational added value;
- native-framework equivalence;
- nonidentifiability;
- redundancy;
- and refusal.

The scientific novelty then depends on whether that ladder produces useful, reproducible distinctions across genuinely different physical/biological domains.

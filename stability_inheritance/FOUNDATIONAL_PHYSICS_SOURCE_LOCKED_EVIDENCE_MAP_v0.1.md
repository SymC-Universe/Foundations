\[

\]

\[
\frac{d}{d\chi}\left[\frac{1}{\chi(1+\chi^2)}\right]

-\frac{1+3\chi^2}{\chi^2(1+\chi^2)^2}<0.
\]

Therefore the displayed function has no interior maximum at \(\chi=1\). The legacy optimization conclusion is not retained.

This does not affect the repeated-root/pole-coalescence result because that result follows from the generator discriminant rather than the information-efficiency functional.

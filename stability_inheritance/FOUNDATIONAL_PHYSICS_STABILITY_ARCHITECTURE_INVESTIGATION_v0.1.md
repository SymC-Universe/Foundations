## Governing scientific question

The foundational-physics program is now one investigation:

> Across quantum, field, particle, gravitational, and cosmological systems, which stability-relevant representations are natively licensed, what information is lost or retained when moving among scalar \(\chi\), modal/vector \(\Chi\), and system/conglomerate \(\Chi_{\mathrm{arc}}\), and when does a proposed cross-scale or cross-sector Stability Inheritance relation add information beyond the strongest native theory?

The investigation is permitted to conclude that a native theory is fully sufficient, a scalar is sufficient, a scalar is useful but redundant, modal/vector organization adds information, system/conglomerate organization adds information, apparent cross-sector similarity is only analogy, or a proposed inheritance relation is equivalent, nonidentifiable, or refused.

## Canonical representation architecture

The current program-wide representation rule is

\[
\text{native observables and generators}
\longrightarrow
\left\{
\chi,\ \Chi,\ \Chi_{\mathrm{arc}}
\right\}
\longrightarrow
\text{relations / transfer / coupling / inheritance tests}.
\]

The three representations are complementary, not a mandatory hierarchy.

### Lowercase \(\chi\)

\(\chi\) is reserved for a licensed scalar stability coordinate. The canonical second-order open-mode form remains

\[
\ddot q+\gamma\dot q+\omega^2 q=0,
\qquad
\chi=\frac{\gamma}{2|\omega|},
\]

only when the native dynamics genuinely support that reduction.

The repeated-root / critical-damping boundary at \(\chi=1\) is admitted for a properly identified damped second-order mode. It is not automatically transferable to a generic resonance width ratio \(\Gamma/(2M)\), a first-order decay rate, an unstable/growing mode, cosmological growth roots, a barrier rate, a Liouvillian eigenvalue without the corresponding generator reduction, a phenomenological particle width, or any coordinate merely chosen to resemble the formula.

### Capital \(\Chi\)

\(\Chi\) denotes a natively justified modal/vector stability representation. Examples may include a qualified Bloch-generator eigenspace, a retarded QFT pole/eigenmode structure, Einstein-Boltzmann modes, shear/Weyl/tidal eigenstructure, admitted neutrino density-matrix/Liouvillian modes, or other native particle or field modes.

Generic multivariate data, arbitrary PCA, likelihood vectors, FRFs, or parameter lists are not automatically \(\Chi\).

### \(\Chi_{\mathrm{arc}}\)

\(\Chi_{\mathrm{arc}}\) denotes a separately justified system/conglomerate organization. It must preserve scientifically relevant coupling, constraint, participation, topology, scale, history, or system-level response that is not reducible to a single scalar or one modal basis.

High dimensionality alone is insufficient.

## Scientific question after A0

For a declared lower-level/parent and higher-level/descendant relation, which stability-relevant representation, if any, survives, transforms, reorganizes, or becomes nonidentifiable under embedding, coarse-graining, coupling, or scale transition, and does that relation add measurable value beyond the strongest native model already appropriate to the higher-level task?

## What prior art already establishes

The A0 conglomeration and closest-precedent search show that the following are established and therefore cannot carry Stability Inheritance novelty:

1. subsystem stability/performance properties can compose into global guarantees through small-gain, ISS, passivity, dissipativity, and related compositional control frameworks;
2. component modal structure can be synthesized into assembly dynamics through component-mode synthesis and dynamic substructuring;
3. reduced models can preserve asymptotic stability, trajectories, safety/reachability specifications, or bounded approximation error through stability-preserving model reduction and approximate simulation/bisimulation;
4. unresolved lower-level dynamics can reappear in reduced higher-level models as memory, fluctuating/noise, and effective closure terms through Mori-Zwanzig/generalized-Langevin theory;
5. resilience can be defined through attraction basins, survivability, viability/recovery regimes, and non-exact return;
6. local dynamics and topology can determine or predict network resilience;
7. multiscale biological robustness, allostasis, redundancy, plasticity, and repeated-stress effects are established;
8. micro- and macro-level variables can exhibit upward and downward causation and scale entwinement, including slowed recovery and hysteresis;
9. cross-scale information can improve held-out dynamical prediction and stability in multiscale closure models;
10. low-level and high-level causal models can be linked by exact or approximate transformations with intervention consistency, abstraction error, information loss, and explicit failure of invalid abstractions;
11. the term "inheritance" has prior uses in dynamical-systems and engineering literature.

## Closest-precedent collisions

### Network resilience

Gao, Barzel, and Barabási derive effective network resilience from local dynamics and topology. Moutsinas and Guo show that network-level mean behavior can mask node-level resilience loss and predict node vulnerability under perturbations.

**Novelty removed:** local/network structure influencing resilience; topology as a predictor of higher-level dynamical outcomes.

### Two-way micro-macro dynamics

Harte et al. DyMES explicitly couples micro transition functions with macrostate variables and produces slowed recovery, hysteresis, and spectral reddening relative to a simpler macro model.

**Novelty removed:** micro-macro scale entwinement causing altered recovery/hysteresis.

### Multiscale predictive modeling

Otness, Zanna, and Bruna show held-out multiscale information improving prediction and stability in dynamical closure tasks.

**Novelty removed:** generic cross-scale information improving held-out dynamical prediction.

### Biological lower-to-higher propagation

Weith et al. show genetic perturbations shifting a molecular network state and relating that state to a heat-stress recovery phenotype, with targeted genetic intervention in a related validation setting.

**Novelty removed:** lower-level biological perturbations reorganizing global network state and affecting recovery-related traits.

### Causal abstraction

Rubenstein et al., Beckers/Halpern, Beckers/Eberhardt/Halpern, Zennaro et al., Rischel/Weichwald, and related causal-abstraction work formalize low/high model transformations, intervention consistency, approximation error, information loss, compositional transformation error, and failure of invalid mappings.

**Novelty removed:** generic intervention-consistent low/high mapping, abstraction error, information-loss measurement, or refusal of inconsistent abstraction.

### Approximate bisimulation / stability-preserving reduction

Approximate simulation/bisimulation and stability-preserving MOR formalize bounded dynamical equivalence and preservation of system properties across abstractions and reductions, including compositional interconnected systems.

**Novelty removed:** generic preservation of stability/dynamics/specifications across abstraction.

## Deep-search result

The targeted deep search "SI exact cross-level comparator collision" did not identify a single prior framework that combines all of the following in one stability-specific empirical qualification:

- independently characterized lower-level stability representation before target exposure;
- separate higher-level/conglomerate representation;
- explicit χ / Χ / Χ_sys representation distinction where scientifically applicable;
- strongest native higher-level comparator;
- closure/memory comparator where applicable;
- explicit carrier/transformation correspondence;
- prospective held-out perturbation/recovery or stability target;
- intervention/counterfactual on the proposed carrier;
- scrambled, generic, same-spectrum, or alternative-carrier specificity controls;
- explicit outcomes for preservation, transformation, modal-only survival, system-only organization, nonidentifiability, native-model sufficiency, refusal, conditional inheritance, and carrier-resolved inheritance.

This absence is a search result, not proof of uniqueness. Later literature can narrow or eliminate the residual under the GOM later-literature rule.

## Residual novelty classification

### Primary residual: NEW_INTEGRATION

The strongest supported residual is a stability-specific qualification architecture that keeps scalar χ, base modal/vector Χ, and separate conglomerate/system Χ_sys distinct, requires native-model-first admission, compares against appropriate compositional/closure/abstraction baselines, and treats refusal/nonidentifiability as first-class results.

### Secondary residual: NEW_DISCRIMINATING_TEST

A candidate new test structure remains:

1. independently characterize lower-level χ and/or Χ;
2. independently characterize the higher-level native state/Χ_sys;
3. freeze the cross-level mapping and target before decisive evidence;
4. compare the strongest native higher-level model against the same task augmented with lower-level information;
5. if incremental value survives, test carrier intervention/counterfactual;
6. test specificity against scrambled/generic/same-spectrum/alternative carriers.

No single source found in the present search combined this complete sequence for a stability/recovery task.

### Secondary residual: NEW_BOUNDARY_TEST

The architecture can contribute by locating where scalar, modal, conglomerate, or cross-level lineage ceases to be adequate, including:

- scalar preserved but modal/conglomerate relation lost;
- modal/subspace relation preserved while scalar changes;
- modal relation preserved but conglomerate reorganization breaks system-level correspondence;
- conglomerate function preserved despite substantial lower-level reorganization;
- native/closure model sufficient, leaving no added SI value;
- nonidentifiable parent/carrier;
- no admissible mapping.

## Provisional SI definition after A0

> Stability Inheritance is the qualification of whether a declared stability-relevant representation or response capacity from one level retains a prospectively identifiable, nonredundant relation to a descendant or higher-level stability task after embedding, coupling, coarse-graining, or scale transition, beyond the strongest native and closure/abstraction models appropriate to that task.

This definition is operational and provisional. It does not assert that inheritance exists in every system.

## Mechanistic ceiling

Incremental predictive value is not sufficient for mechanistic inheritance. Carrier-resolved inheritance requires the existing six-gate evidence ladder:

1. independent source characterization;
2. explicit correspondence;
3. frozen transformation rule;
4. prospective target prediction;
5. intervention/counterfactual;
6. specificity against scrambled/generic/alternative carriers.

Causal abstraction, approximate bisimulation, and native domain theory are comparator frameworks, not evidence to be relabeled as SI.

# Stability Inheritance Physical Hierarchy Plan Delta v0.1

**From:** PHYSICAL_HIERARCHY_PLAN_PACKET_v0.1.md

**Authority:** APQ ledger v0.1  
**Status:** REVISION APPLIED / NOT FROZEN

The APQ review found that v0.1 could not support a novel empirical inheritance claim because standard component-mode synthesis and experimental dynamic substructuring already predict component-to-assembly behavior. The first physical cycle is therefore reclassified as a **P0-Q known-truth physical qualification benchmark**.

Material changes:

1. **Claim ceiling lowered.** First cycle cannot promote CARRIER_RESOLVED_INHERITANCE or P1.
2. **Χ_con made independent.** Ground-truth Χ_con is constructed only from directly measured assembled-system outcomes after component predictions are frozen.
3. **Comparator purpose clarified.** Scalar-only, modal-only, and SI representations are benchmarked against CMS/state-space substructuring; this is not the future macro-only versus macro-plus-lower P1.
4. **Q3 damping intervention removed from first cycle.** Damping-specific work becomes a later extension after independent cross-talk calibration.
5. **Interface integrity gates added.** Attachment mass, preload, coupling stiffness, repeatability, and post-disassembly parent drift must be quantified.
6. **Physical-model gates added.** Reciprocity, passivity/dissipation, coupling-rank, sensor-health, and conditioning checks precede interpretation.
7. **Crowded-mode rule tightened.** Principal-angle/subspace correspondence is primary in near-degenerate sectors.
8. **Framework failure states added.** FRAMEWORK_NO_ADDED_VALUE and FRAMEWORK_NOT_OPERATIONAL are admissible.
9. **Physical specificity strengthened.** Alternate interface is the physical carrier control; label scrambling remains code validation only.
10. **Promotion requires later independent evidence.** No transport/generalization claim from one apparatus.

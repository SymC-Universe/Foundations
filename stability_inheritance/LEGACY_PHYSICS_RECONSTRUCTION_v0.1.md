## Candidate A: SymC Noughts

**Legacy title:** *SymC Noughts: Understanding the Electromagnetic Vacuum as a Physical Substrate*

### Legacy object

The old manuscript treats the electromagnetic vacuum as a physical photon substrate, characterizes the lossless Maxwell wave equation as χ = 0, interprets vacuum impedance as a substrate property, and links electroweak symmetry breaking, renormalization-group evolution, and later electromagnetic constants through an inheritance narrative.

### Reconstruction

- **χ:** Maxwell vacuum wave propagation admits no damping term in the ideal source-free wave equation, but this by itself does not establish an inherited stability coordinate across symmetry breaking.
- **Χ:** native modal/gauge-field structure must be reconstructed from Standard Model electroweak and QED dynamics rather than from the old oscillator analogy.
- **Χ_con:** any later electromagnetic system-level organization must be defined separately from the base gauge/modal structure.
- **Candidate carrier:** not assumed. A physical carrier/transformation would need to be identified through electroweak symmetry breaking, field mixing, matching conditions, running couplings, or another native mechanism.
- **Primary native comparator:** Standard Model electroweak symmetry breaking plus QED/EFT/RG description.

### Residual inheritance question

Does an independently identified electroweak parent structure transmit a measurable, nonredundant constraint into later electromagnetic organization that is not already exhausted by standard gauge symmetry breaking, parameter matching, and RG evolution?

### Refusal conditions

Return **NO_ADDED_INHERITANCE_VALUE** if standard electroweak/QED/EFT machinery fully specifies the descendant relation and the inheritance representation contributes no independent predictive, identifiability, boundary, or intervention value.

Return **CARRIER_NOT_IDENTIFIED** if the proposed substrate is only a relabeling of the electromagnetic vacuum, vacuum impedance, or unit-dependent constants without a frozen parent-to-child physical transformation.

### Current disposition

**High-risk direct SI candidate. Rebuild essentially from zero.** It is valuable precisely because it can falsify the temptation to call every background structure a substrate.

## Candidate B: Neutrino matter-induced dephasing

**Legacy/revised title:** *Density-Dependent Matter-Induced Dephasing in Neutrino Oscillations with Preserved Vacuum Unitarity*

### Legacy object

The revised public manuscript models three-flavor oscillations with a density-dependent matter dephasing term, preserves exact vacuum unitarity by taking vacuum damping to zero, and defines mode-specific χ_k from diagonal elements of a damping operator in the mass basis relative to beat frequencies.

### Reconstruction

- **χ:** χ_k is only admissible if the effective reduced generator and damping/frequency assignment are independently licensed for the declared mode and regime. It is not the neutrino stability architecture.
- **Χ:** the native modal object is richer: mass/flavor basis structure, PMNS mixing, matter-dependent Hamiltonian, environmental/dephasing operator, eigenvectors/eigenspaces, basis rotation, and any non-normal or near-degenerate structure.
- **Χ_con:** system-level behavior includes coupled flavor-transition probabilities, coherence/decoherence across the full three-mode system, path-dependent matter profiles, and any collective/environmental organization beyond one modal quotient.
- **Candidate carrier:** matter profile/environmental channel only if it carries a frozen parent-to-descendant relation rather than merely acting as an instantaneous parameter.

### Residual inheritance question

After a medium or dynamical regime changes, does independently characterized prior modal organization Χ constrain the descendant Χ or Χ_con beyond what the instantaneous standard oscillation-plus-matter variables already predict?

### Strong null / refusal

If the descendant state is fully determined by the current Hamiltonian, matter potential, initial quantum state, and standard decoherence variables, with no additional predictive contribution from the proposed prior carrier/history, classify **ENVIRONMENTAL_REORGANIZATION_WITHOUT_INHERITANCE**.

If scalar χ_k changes while a modal/subspace correspondence survives, retain the modal result without promoting scalar inheritance.

### Current disposition

**Bridge and adversarial refusal candidate.** Neutrinos are especially useful because ordinary basis rotation and medium dependence can imitate "inheritance" unless the prior-state contribution is independently identified.

## Candidate C: Black-hole ringdown

**Legacy/revised title:** *Structural Mapping of Linear Damping Operators Across Cosmological Growth and Black Hole Ringdown*

### Legacy object

The revised manuscript already moved toward a spectrum-first treatment. It defines an observational ringdown ratio from quasinormal-mode frequency components and emphasizes Kerr spectral reorganization, mode families, overtones, spin dependence, and relaxation times.

### Reconstruction

How much of the higher-dimensional ringdown Χ and waveform-level Χ_con is retained or destroyed by a scalar spectral descriptor, and is any information about the progenitor or merger history inherited into the observable ringdown beyond the no-hair/remnant-parameter baseline?

### Refusal conditions

If final remnant parameters plus standard perturbation theory account for the relevant ringdown observables and progenitor-level information adds no independent predictive value, classify **NO_RINGDOWN_INHERITANCE_FOR_DECLARED_TASK**.

Scalar spectral similarity without mode-family/excitation correspondence is not inheritance.

## Representation rule

The three project representation levels remain distinct:

- \(\chi\): an admitted scalar stability coordinate only where native dynamics and the declared task license it.
- \(\Chi\): an admitted modal/vector representation only where a physical or otherwise scientifically justified vector/state object exists.
- \(\Chi_{\mathrm{arc}}\): a separately justified system/conglomerate representation, never inferred merely because a multivariable predictor performs better.

Generic FRFs, likelihood vectors, feature sets, or concatenated predictors are not automatically renamed \(\Chi\) or \(\Chi_{\mathrm{arc}}\). Stability Inheritance is not a fourth representation. It is a qualified relation among independently admitted source/target representations, carrier/context, and realized behavior.

## Cross-domain relations that survive

### Scalar compression is conditional, not privileged

Closed evidence supports distinct outcomes: useful-but-redundant local \(\chi\) in BRB, information-losing scalar compression in DESI, and scalar refusal/non-admission in F-16, pyFBS, and BARC. The program therefore asks whether a scalar is adequate for the declared task rather than assuming scalar sufficiency.

### Vector/modal information can survive while exact coordinates reorganize

BRB directly admits a physical modal subspace whose dominant skeleton persists while fine geometry rotates with amplitude. F-16 and BARC support context-conditioned multichannel/modal organization but do not automatically admit project \(\Chi\). DESI Result 2 establishes only a statistical response vector whose composition matters, deliberately not a physical modal \(\Chi\).

### System-level organization cannot be inferred from dimensionality alone

Across F-16, BARC, BRB, and DESI, multicomponent or history-sensitive behavior is common, but no lane automatically produces a unique \(\Chi_{\mathrm{arc}}\). A formal \(\Chi_{\mathrm{arc}}\) requires an independently justified system-level object with declared physical or functional semantics and a reproducible task.

### Context, interface, and history repeatedly condition realized behavior

The closed measured evidence identifies domain-native conditioning through F-16 interface regime and excitation family, pyFBS component/assembly correspondence, BARC boundary impedance and attachment realization, BRB amplitude and wear/contact history, and DESI distributed tracer-response organization. The recurrence is architectural, but the mechanisms remain native.

### Native-framework equivalence is a successful SI outcome

pyFBS is the cleanest example: the architecture relation is real and measured, yet native SVT/LM-FBS already explains it. BARC and BRB wear likewise have sufficient native engineering explanations. SI must therefore allow ARCHITECTURE_PRESENT / SI_ADDED_VALUE_NOT_REQUIRED as a legitimate closure.

## Current SI interpretation

The closed evidence does not support “stability is always inherited” as a law. It supports a narrower, falsifiable architecture: task-dependent representations can preserve, lose, or reorganize information; coupling, embedding, regime, and history can condition realized higher-level behavior; native theory can remain sufficient; and an inheritance relation must earn nonredundant value beyond native and additive explanations while retaining explicit refusal states.

The working SI object is consequently a qualification architecture for preservation, transformation, redundancy, context dependence, and refusal across representations, not a universal force or scalar law.

## Independence consequence

Existing Chemistry, GRI, Bio Chi, Market, Foundations synthetic, and legacy-physics results may inform the SI hypothesis and qualification machinery. They cannot independently confirm the newly narrowed cross-level relation if their outcomes were already known when that relation was formulated.

The first future SI portfolio should therefore be built from tests whose decisive evidence remains untouched after the exact cross-level claim, comparator, carrier definition, χ/Χ/Χ_con mapping, refusal rule, and outcome metric are frozen.

## Provisional portfolio logic, not a freeze

A scientifically strong minimum portfolio would benefit from materially different mechanisms:

1. a controlled physical hierarchy for causal carrier/intervention specificity;
2. a biological perturbation/recovery system for adaptive and reorganizing Χ_con;
3. a market or grid system for naturally occurring multiscale organization and external transfer.

This is a design hypothesis only. P0-N/A0 and APQ must close independently for each domain before portfolio composition is frozen.

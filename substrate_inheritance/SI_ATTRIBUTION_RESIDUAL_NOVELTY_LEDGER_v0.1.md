## Rule

Credit follows the work. SI does not claim ownership of established mathematics, methods, or physical phenomena. A future novelty statement may cover only the residual contribution left after the nearest legitimate prior work is identified and credited.

`NO_CLOSE_PRECEDENT_FOUND` will mean only that a good-faith search did not locate a close precedent. It will not be treated as proof of novelty.

## Prohibited novelty language before collision

Do not claim:

- first use of Schur complements for coupled systems;
- first use of Green functions or self-energy for substrates;
- first modal/eigenvector inheritance concept;
- first use of subspace angles for mode matching;
- first use of left/right eigenvectors in non-normal systems;
- first observation that coupling changes a child/system response;
- first multi-parent interaction decomposition;
- first hierarchical system reduction;
- first recovery/resilience analysis of coupled systems;
- first scalar/modal/system description merely because those words are combined.

## Candidate residual contribution classes

A future SI contribution may survive as one or more of:

- a stricter prospective evidence architecture distinguishing influence from inherited carrier structure;
- a fail-closed integration of carrier correspondence, perturbation, coupling specificity, uncertainty, and refusal;
- a quantified map of where parent architecture remains predictive after coupling and where it ceases to be;
- a hierarchical-closure test tied specifically to prospectively inherited quantities;
- a usable ingestion/analysis tool that adds measurable value over the strongest native method for a frozen task;
- a new empirical prediction that survives untouched prospective testing.

These are possibilities only. They are not novelty conclusions.

## Status vocabulary

- `CLOSED`: declared question answered at its present epistemic level.
- `OPEN_ACTIVE`: can be advanced now without waiting for a new physical result.
- `WAITING_ON_SOURCE`: valid next step depends on an upstream/admissible evidence object that does not yet exist.
- `REFUSED`: current evidence cannot support the requested object under frozen rules.
- `PLANNED_COMPUTE`: scientific computation remains prospective and is not yet a result.
- `NOT_APPLICABLE`: the object is not required at the current stage.

## 3. Method vulnerabilities and their current disposition

### Scalar nonidentifiability

Status: `CLOSED_AS_DEMONSTRATED_SYNTHETIC_LIMIT`.

Distinct coupling geometries can share one scalar response condition. This blocks any claim that one scalar match identifies the underlying conglomerative relation.

Reopen only if a future proposed scalar claims to preserve additional architecture under a stated derivation; then test that scalar prospectively.

### Individual-mode fragility near degeneracy

Status: `CLOSED_AS_METHOD_RULE`.

Individual carrier identity can degrade while the invariant subspace remains stable. Current rule: retain subspace/projector geometry rather than force one-to-one vectors.

### Sequential normalized lineage-flow information loss

Status: `CLOSED_AS_NEGATIVE_METHOD_RESULT`.

Flow remains descriptive. It cannot replace direct end-to-end carrier/subspace comparison.

### Finite-depth first-crossing convergence

Status: `CLOSED_AS_INVALID_SHORTCUT`.

A first favorable depth crossing is insufficient. Sustained depth behavior is required in the current synthetic reduction task.

### Approximate hierarchical reduction as universal closure

Status: `REFUSED`.

Approximate methods can fail sharply in selected regimes. Closure must remain quantity- and regime-specific.

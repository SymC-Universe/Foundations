Targeted failure modes:

### Q2: ensemble discriminability and coupling specificity

Targeted failure modes:
- a favorable single synthetic example;
- scalar-spectrum equivalence masquerading as carrier inheritance;

- retain coupling architecture as a separate object.

### Q3: single-response nonidentifiability

Targeted failure mode:
- one matched scalar child response being mistaken for a unique parent-child coupling architecture.

Observed qualification consequence:

- second-condition/frequency information and carrier/coupling structure remain necessary where applicable.

### Q4: coordinate and degeneracy robustness

Targeted failure modes:
- coordinate representation artifacts;
- forced one-to-one eigenvector identity in crowded/degenerate sectors.

Change pressure generated:
- use invariant subspaces/projectors where individual modes are not stable;
- keep coordinate invariance as a required property of promoted carrier claims.

### Q5: substrate-depth convergence

Targeted failure mode:
- treating arbitrary finite retained depth as physically sufficient.

Change pressure generated:
- compare finite-depth embedding against an independent converged/semi-infinite reference where constructible;
- treat depth as task/regime dependent rather than a universal inheritance length.

### Q6: correspondence-strength failure boundary

Targeted failure mode:

Change pressure generated:

- do not convert the synthetic strength axis into a physical inheritance coordinate.

### Q7: non-normal and near-defective geometry

### Q8: separate electronic channel

## Current stopping rationale

Candidate v0.4 is not proposed for P1 merely because the software validates. P0-Q architecture qualification can pause when:

1. the v0.4 semantic guards pass CI;
2. no unresolved known-bad case can silently produce a promoted interpretation;
3. the Function/Limit P0-D program has tested hierarchical closure and ordinary supported regions sufficiently to identify the next narrow physical question;
4. the experimental-opportunity/literature collision has identified the strongest native comparator/method and residual empirical question;
5. a provenance-complete real-system input can enter without changing its upstream science to favor SI.

Only then should a narrow P1 claim be considered, with a complete new MFR-14 record and untouched decisive evidence.

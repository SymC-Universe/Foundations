## Attribution rule

Credit follows the work. SI does not gain novelty because established methods are assembled in one repository or renamed with SI terminology. The scientific contribution, if any, is the residual contribution left after the strongest relevant native methods and prior physical demonstrations are accounted for.

This ledger does not certify publication-level novelty. It constrains what the current manuscript is allowed to say.

## 1. Established native methods and phenomena removed from SI novelty

### Modal correlation, mode tracking, and clustered-subspace handling

**Status:** `ESTABLISHED_NATIVE_METHOD`.

SI cannot claim novelty for:

- MAC/CMAC-like vector correlation;
- one-to-one mode tracking;
- clustered-eigenvalue/subspace tracking;
- uncertainty-aware modal correspondence;
- targeted adsorbate vibrational mode tracking.

The v0.2 collision adds Herrmann and Reiher (2006) as direct surface/adsorbate mode-tracking precedent.

### Clean versus adsorbate-covered surface vibrational comparison

**Status:** `ESTABLISHED_PHYSICAL_ANALYSIS_CLASS`.

Clean and covered surface phonon dispersions, interface modes, overlayer modes, and modes related to pristine surfaces have long been compared experimentally and computationally. SI may not claim novelty for descriptive parent/covered-system mode relatedness.

### Structural condensation, dynamic substructuring, and CMS

**Status:** `ESTABLISHED_NATIVE_METHOD_FAMILY`.

SI cannot claim novelty for:

- dividing a system into substructures;
- exact or approximate component reduction;
- coupling independently modeled components;
- response-preserving reduced-order modeling;
- uncertainty propagation from components to an assembly;
- tracing multilevel vibration transmission paths.

The v0.2 collision adds modern response-dependent CMS and multilevel DS/CMS Transfer Path Analysis as especially close neighboring method families.

### Input-output and response-focused reduction

**Status:** `ESTABLISHED_NATIVE_METHOD_FAMILY`.

Task-dependent response preservation is not uniquely SI. Future hierarchical-closure claims must identify what SI-specific information is being preserved beyond ordinary response reduction.

### Surface Green functions and embedding

**Status:** `ESTABLISHED_NATIVE_METHOD`.

Semi-infinite substrate embedding, surface Green functions, and self-energy are inherited mathematics.

### Adsorbate-substrate vibrational hybridization and coupling

**Status:** `ESTABLISHED_PHYSICAL_PHENOMENON`.

SI may not claim that substrate modes can mix with, broaden, shift, or contribute to adsorbate modes as a new physical discovery.

### Isotope controls and substrate perturbation response

**Status:** `ESTABLISHED_EXPERIMENTAL_METHOD/PHENOMENON`.

Isotopic mixtures have been used to separate coupling and chemical/frequency shifts, and substrate excitation followed by adsorbate response has been measured. These become inherited controls.

### Higher-order subset/Mobius source decomposition

**Status:** `ESTABLISHED_MATHEMATICS; PHYSICAL_SI_APPLICATION_UNRESOLVED`.

The decomposition itself is not an SI invention.

This is presently a **synthetic methodological result**, not a physical novelty claim.

**Current status:** `SYNTHETIC_FORMAL_QUALIFICATION_ONLY`.

### R5. Function/Limit map of inheritance-like correspondence

The program jointly maps ordinary functioning interiors and limits rather than optimizing a single pass/fail score. Candidate contribution lies in applying that dual-map discipline to a prospectively frozen inheritance evidence architecture while retaining negative and refused states.

**Current status:** `FM1_FM4_COMPLETE_SYNTHETIC; FM5_PENDING`.

## 5. What would materially strengthen the residual novelty case

The residual contribution becomes substantially stronger only if at least one of the following is earned prospectively:

1. a physical parent architecture predicts carrier/subspace transformation in an independent child better than the strongest task-matched native comparator;
2. a frozen parent intervention produces a child response that survives specificity controls and cannot be reduced to generic transfer-path influence;
3. the SI refusal/uncertainty architecture prevents a false physical inference that a standard scalar/frequency-only workflow would make, on a prospectively frozen task;
4. a hierarchical physical test shows that an inherited carrier relation preserves a declared higher-level quantity in some regime and fails in another, with the boundary predicted rather than selected post hoc;
5. an untouched P1 prediction survives MFR-14.

## 6. What would narrow or eliminate the residual novelty case

The residual claim should shrink if deeper prior art reveals an existing method or experiment implementing an equivalent full prospective sequence, or if future fair comparison shows that standard native methods already answer the frozen scientific task equally well without the SI-specific architecture.

That outcome would not invalidate the synthetic results. It would change attribution and the publication claim.

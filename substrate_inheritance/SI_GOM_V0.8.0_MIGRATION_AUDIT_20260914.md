## 1. GOM v0.8.0 controls already represented in SI

The following program-level controls are already materially implemented in the active SI development branch.

### Native-model-first and fail-closed abstraction

SI does not begin from a preferred scalar. It begins from the native governing object, physical carrier, coupling structure, and declared parent/child relation. Mechanical chi is optional and admissible only when its own dynamical reduction is licensed.

### Starting representations are not an exhaustive ontology

Scalar, modal/vector/subspace, and conglomeration/system organization remain useful starting reporting slots. They are not a complete decomposition of chi and are not a literal formula for chi. `NOT_APPLICABLE` and an open channel remain permitted.

### Local identity is distinct from embedded realized behavior

SI separately records local/intrinsic organization, outward coupling, environmental/system transformation, feedback/return, and embedded/closed-loop behavior where the native science supports them.

### Participation, observability, identifiability, estimate, placement, uncertainty, and admission remain separate

A participating carrier need not be identifiable; an identifiable coordinate need not be precisely estimated; an estimate need not support regime placement; and weak observability is not physical absence.

### Function Map and Limit Map are coequal

FM1-FM4 map ordinary functioning interiors and limits. Negative method results remain part of the result rather than being repaired away.

### Hierarchical closure is quantity- and regime-specific

A grouped subsystem may be reused at a higher level only to the extent that it preserves the declared quantities required by that higher-level question over the declared validity regime.

### Recovery/resilience is separate from state classification

Asymptotic return is not equated with finite-time resilience. Non-normal transient amplification, reorganization, nonreturn, and refusal remain admissible outcomes.

### Cross-scale recurrence does not imply common mechanism or universality

Repeated mathematical motif, preserved architecture under grouping, shared physical mechanism, and a common numerical chi region are separate claim classes.

### Experimental opportunity precedes targeted literature collision

`SI_EXPERIMENTAL_OPPORTUNITY_v0.1.md` was frozen before the targeted literature search. The literature collision narrowed the residual question and inherited established native methods instead of converting prior art into claimed novelty.

### Attribution follows residual contribution

`SI_ATTRIBUTION_RESIDUAL_NOVELTY_LEDGER_v0.2.md` already removes mode tracking, component-mode synthesis, Green-function embedding, generic hybridization, isotope controls, and related native methods from any SI novelty claim.

### Refusal and negative evidence are valid outputs

Scalar nonidentifiability, lineage-flow information loss, approximate closure failure, non-monotonic finite-depth behavior, missing physical input, and unresolved carrier identity remain reportable results.

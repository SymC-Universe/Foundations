The existing V1-V9 synthetic/mathematical/software qualification, real-system evidence firewall, correspondence protocol, and all physical-evidence ceilings remain unchanged.

v0.8.3 adds two important interpretation controls for this project:

1. If a carrier-resolved local/modal chi is licensed and a broader coupled-system Chi architecture is also represented, the relationship between them must be tested rather than presumed.
2. Perturbation/intervention may provide evidence about carrier influence or inherited organization, but recovery is a separate outcome dimension. A child system returning after perturbation does not by itself prove inheritance, and inherited correspondence does not by itself imply recovery.

Where applicable, intervention records should distinguish resistance, finite-time response, first reclaim, sustained recovery, reorganization, basin robustness, and repeated-perturbation behavior.

Physical inheritance still requires the already-frozen prospective parent/child correspondence, prediction, intervention/counterfactual and specificity controls. No chemistry HOLD or development result is promoted by this migration.

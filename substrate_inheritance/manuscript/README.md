## Editing rules

1. The manuscript may be updated in meaningful batches rather than on every commit.
2. Every material scientific statement must remain within the status earned by its source evidence.
3. Synthetic P0-D/P0-Q evidence must not be phrased as physical substrate-inheritance evidence.
4. Negative results, refusals, nonidentifiability, and method failures remain part of the manuscript rather than being edited away for narrative smoothness.
5. Established native methods remain credited as native methods. The manuscript may claim only the residual contribution supported after prior-art collision.
6. The manuscript must distinguish substrate influence, conditional inheritance, and substrate inheritance.
7. Scalar, modal/subspace, and conglomeration/system descriptions are complementary starting representations, not a complete decomposition of chi and not an omnibus score.
8. A collection of lower-level chi values never becomes a system chi by averaging or convenience.
9. Local dynamical identity and embedded realized behavior remain separate unless a justified closure relation connects them.
10. Any future P1 physical claim must be frozen prospectively under the applicable MFR-14 requirements before decisive evidence is opened.

## Manuscript promotion rule

Before any manuscript freeze, every material quantitative or interpretive statement should map to one of the rows above or to a later version of this matrix. New result classes require an explicit source-of-record row before they are treated as settled manuscript content.

## High-risk phrases to reject during editing

- “proves substrate inheritance” for synthetic results;
- “universal” without independent cross-domain confirmation;
- “first demonstration” without an exhaustive legitimate prior-art basis;
- “inheritance length” for FM2 effective depth;
- “lineage loss” for sequential normalized-flow disagreement when direct geometry reconverges;
- “system chi” derived by averaging or combining local chi values without an independent governing derivation;
- “exceptional point” inferred from scalar proximity alone;
- “causal fraction” or “inheritance percentage” for row-normalized descriptive lineage flow;
- “probability” for seeded perturbation envelopes not calibrated as probabilities.

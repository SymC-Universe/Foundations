# SI ERIES-RESPOND Candidate Audit

Date: 2026-09-28
GOM: v0.8.8
Stage: P0-N / P0-Q candidate-screen only

ERIES-RESPOND is scientifically useful as a hierarchical-embedding development benchmark because it includes forced-vibration tests of a 2x2 pile-group foundation and of the EuroProteas structure resting on the same foundation, with soil, pile, foundation, and roof instrumentation across approximately 1-10 Hz.

A 2025 open-access conference paper by Di Laora et al. (DOI 10.1007/978-3-031-98893-6_13) describes the same campaign, including three stages: foundation only, structure installed with excitation at the foundation, and excitation at the structure top. The paper also states the project objective of testing the effect of pile-foundation impedance on superstructure response and publishes selected preliminary results.

Disposition:
- P0-D / P0-Q development and native-comparator use: ACCEPT.
- Globally untouched P1 assumption: REFUSE.
- Endpoint-specific untouched P1 eligibility: NEED_MORE_INFO and requires proof that the exact decisive endpoint was not published, plotted, summarized, or used in tuning before any target values are opened.

Use ERIES-RESPOND for Function/Limit mapping, SSI/substructure comparator qualification, amplitude dependence, hierarchical closure, uncertainty, and refusal behavior. Continue searching for a cleaner physical P1 target. Do not open decisive embedded-system values merely to determine eligibility.

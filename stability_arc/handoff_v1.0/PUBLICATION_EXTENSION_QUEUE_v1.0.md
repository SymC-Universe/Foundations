## Priority 1: finite-temperature generalized amplitude damping

### Purpose

Change the dissipative generator in a way that cannot be described as merely adding another dephasing rate.

Use both downward relaxation and upward thermal excitation.

### Why it matters

The current dephasing extension still lives in a closely related two-level open-system family. Finite-temperature amplitude damping changes the stationary state and the balance of dissipative channels.

- measurement operator family;

- quotient/admissibility rule;
- scalar-modal report;
- full mean-square classifier;
- refusal conditions;
- primary falsification hypothesis.

### Why it matters

A scientifically valuable outcome may be:

- a higher-dimensional invariant architecture;
- a different admissible quotient;
- a robust refusal state;
- `NONIDENTIFIABLE`.

- which scalar invariants are allowed to accompany the vector/modal structure;

- explicit refusal conditions.

## Stop conditions

Before publication, stop and reassess if:

- the independent generator invalidates the core assignment architecture;
- no reproducible mode correspondence can be defined;
- numerical classification becomes tolerance-dependent without a stable uncertainty treatment;
- the only surviving claim is a same-family pattern;
- a proposed scalar is useful only after outcome-driven tuning.

Such outcomes should narrow the manuscript rather than be hidden.

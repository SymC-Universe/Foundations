## Step 4: choose a genuinely independent next family

The completed H11/H12 dephasing family should not be repeated merely to increase N.

Choose and document a new physical extension, preferably one of:

- finite-temperature generalized amplitude damping;
- non-planar measurement without guaranteed 1D dark factor;
- another independently motivated generator family.

## Step 5: freeze before view

Before new outcome generation, persist:

- scientific question;
- exact equations/conventions;
- parameter range;
- fresh seed or source partition;
- admissibility and refusal rules;
- scalar-modal report fields;
- primary and secondary gates;
- stop conditions;

Only then launch the calculation.

## Step 6: failures remain evidence

If the new phase fails:

- preserve the result;
- classify the failure;
- inspect mechanism without changing the registered score;
- derive a new hypothesis only in a new version;
- use fresh evidence for confirmation.

Do not rescue the phase by changing thresholds or filtering cases after reveal.

- GFSA external-candidate contract recovery;

## Step 8: restarting continuous compute

Once a new preregistration is frozen, re-enable or update the Stability Arc compute controller to:

`freeze -> run -> audit -> preserve -> advance`

It may autonomously repair mechanical CI issues only when frozen science and conclusions cannot change.

## Completion signal

A future extension campaign is ready for manuscript assembly when it has:

- at least one genuinely independent physical extension;
- preserved failure lineage;
- independent-engine validation;
- a frozen reproducibility package;
- no unresolved provenance issue that is material to the claims being submitted.

# Research Community Code of Conduct

The purpose of this repository is rigorous, reproducible scientific exchange. Participation is welcome from people who agree to engage with the work and with one another professionally.

## Expected conduct

Participants should:

- critique claims, methods, equations, code, and evidence rather than attacking people;
- distinguish demonstrated errors from disagreements in interpretation;
- provide reproducible evidence or clear reasoning when making technical claims;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes when they are identified;
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, or unwanted disclosure of personal information;
- avoid spam, promotional flooding, fabricated citations, or knowingly false technical claims;
- respect the difference between hypothesis generation and established evidence;
- preserve a good-faith scientific record, including negative results and corrections.

## Scientific disagreement

Strong disagreement is acceptable and often useful. It should remain specific enough to evaluate. A technically critical issue is most useful when it identifies the affected claim or computation, explains the problem, and provides a derivation, source, counterexample, or reproducible test when possible.

## Unacceptable conduct

Maintainers may remove or restrict contributions that contain harassment, threats, targeted abuse, private information, repeated bad-faith disruption, or content unrelated to the scientific or technical purpose of the repository.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this policy. Moderation decisions should be proportionate, documented when appropriate, and separated from judgments about whether a scientific claim is correct.

Good-faith criticism of SymC, including criticism that weakens or falsifies a current hypothesis, is explicitly within scope and should not be treated as a conduct violation merely because it is unfavorable to the project.

**Classification: SELF / FOUNDATION, not external collision.**

Provides the explicit damping ratio, exceptional-point interpretation, flat-LambdaCDM acceleration identity, an offset prediction for extensions, and a leading-order substrate-inheritance proposal. It does not itself establish a general modal \(\Chi\) architecture for the full DM/DE problem.

### Nadkarni-Ghosh & Refregier, Einstein-Boltzmann eigenanalysis (2016/2017)

**Classification: PARTIAL COLLISION.**

Strong overlap:
- non-autonomous Einstein-Boltzmann system analyzed through eigenvalues;
- real-to-complex eigenvalue transition marking onset of oscillations;
- dimensionless \(\epsilon=k/(aH)\);
- stiffness ratio from eigenvalue real parts;
- mapped regimes across the \(k-a\) plane.

Non-overlap:
- no damping-ratio / EP coordinate;
- transition is primarily radiation/matter/mode-scale driven, not a joint DM/DE architecture;
- no capital-\(\Chi\)-type relational state;
- no SA/SI interpretation;
- scientific goal is perturbation-solver mathematics, not dark-sector organization.

This is the strongest modal prior art and must be treated as a mandatory comparator.

**Classification: PARTIAL COLLISION.**

# Cosmology RQE Metric Policy Freeze v0.1

**Status:** PROSPECTIVE POLICY, RUN-INDEPENDENT  
**Date:** 2026-09-30  
**Purpose:** Freeze the metrics and comparison logic used by the Representation Qualification Engine before new held-out results are inspected.

## F1 — local \(\chi\) qualification

Primary question: is the local \(\chi\)-class coordinate independently informative, or only a useful coordinate generated by the native model?

Required checks:
1. algebraic/native-model dependence;
2. posterior correlation with the native parameter(s) that generate it;
3. predictive increment for the frozen target \(Y\).

Allowed classifications:
- independent;
- coordinate_only;
- refused.

A coordinate_only result does not terminate the architecture. It prohibits treating local \(\chi\) as an independent degree of freedom.

Current established example: DESI DR2 BAO shows \(\Delta z\) in constant-\(w\)CDM is strongly tied to \(w\) (\(r\approx-0.97\)), while flat-\(\Lambda\)CDM gives the exact identity \(q=0\Longleftrightarrow\chi_\delta=1\). Sources/comparators: [Chr26].

## F2 — modal necessity

The resolved/modal representation must beat the scalar/compressed representation on at least one preregistered held-out target without losing calibration.

Primary metrics:
- effective information dimension
\[
d_{\rm eff}=\frac{(\sum_i\lambda_i)^2}{\sum_i\lambda_i^2}
\]
following the information-mode logic of [Lee26b];
- held-out predictive log score or equivalent;
- summary sufficiency/complementarity metric following [Sui25];
- compression-loss diagnostic following [Hea20].

Promotion rule:
- modal structure is required only when a material second-or-higher information direction is resolved and the resolved representation improves the frozen scientific task beyond the scalar/compressed baseline.

Reference/comparators: [Nad16, Lee26b, Sui25, Hea20, Bas19].

## F3 — aggregate \(\Chi\) necessity

Candidate \(\Chi\) must add information conditional on the qualified local/modal components.

Primary criterion:
\[
\Delta I_\Chi = I(Y;\Chi\mid\chi,\mathcal M,\text{native variables})>0
\]
or an equivalent preregistered held-out score.

Required nulls:
- matched-complexity marginal-only model;
- relation randomization / phase or pairing destruction when scientifically admissible;
- native-parameter augmentation.

Promotion requires a gain beyond dimensionality alone.

Reference/comparators: [Sui25, Nad16].

## F4 — Stability Arc necessity

Compare:
1. endpoint-only state;
2. instantaneous/static state;
3. resolved trajectory.

Primary metric: held-out predictive/discriminating increment of the trajectory representation.

The Arc is refused as a separate layer if endpoints/static state reproduce its full performance.

Reference/comparators: [Chr26, Roy11, Wie10].

## F5 — Stability Inheritance necessity

Test whether an earlier qualified state carries prospective information about a later state after ordinary cosmological history has been conditioned out.

Required comparison set:
- standard transfer/growth history;
- curvature / initial-condition variables where relevant;
- qualified \(\chi,\mathcal M,\Chi\);
- candidate inheritance map.

Primary criterion:
\[
\Delta I_{\rm SI}
=
I(Y_{t+\Delta};\mathcal T_{\rm SI}
\mid
\text{standard history},\chi,\mathcal M,\Chi)>0.
\]

Required null: shuffle or destroy temporal lineage while preserving marginals where scientifically valid.

Reference/comparators: [Vru21, Wie10].

## F6 — joint DM/DE function

The architecture must improve a joint frozen task involving at least one DM-side and one DE-side observable.

DM-side candidates:
- \(f\sigma_8(z)\);
- \(P(k,z)\) / full-shape structure;
- lensing/potential observables when qualified.

DE-side candidates:
- \(H(z)\);
- BAO/distances;
- \(q(z)\) / acceleration transition where admissible.

Required comparator:
- conventional expansion-growth reconstruction.

No unique physical decomposition is inferred unless dark degeneracy is explicitly broken.

Reference/comparators: [Roy11, Sha21, Bas19, Kun07b, Mar19, Per22, You26].

## F7 — adversarial transport

Minimum adversaries, dataset-matched where possible:
- flat \(\Lambda\)CDM / GR;
- \(w_0w_a\) dynamical DE;
- free \(\sum m_\nu\);
- modified gravity;
- interacting dark sector;
- supernova/sample/systematic variation.

Primary requirement: the representation decision itself must remain calibrated under plausible alternative physics and systematics.

Rejection/misspecification comparators:
- posterior-predictive / relative-entropy consistency [Nic18];
- compression-loss controls [Hea20].

## Global refusal rule

If standard existing methods reproduce every representation decision with equal or better held-out calibration and no loss of interpretability, the RQE is classified as scientifically redundant even if its terminology is novel.

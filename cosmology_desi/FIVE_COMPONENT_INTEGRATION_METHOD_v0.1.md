# Five-Component Stability Architecture Integration Method v0.1

**Status:** PROSPECTIVE, SOURCE-GROUNDED, NOT YET EMPIRICALLY PROMOTED  
**Date:** 2026-09-30  
**Components:** local (chi), modal structure, aggregate (Chi), Stability Arc (SA), Stability Inheritance (SI), applied jointly to DM/DE cosmology  
**Rule:** every borrowed concept, comparator, and control remains explicitly referenced. Novelty is claimed only for surviving integration, never for established ingredients.

## 1. Integration target

The working architecture is

[
chi_i(a,k)
longrightarrow
mathcal M(a,k)
longrightarrow
Chi(a)
longrightarrow
mathcal A_{Chi}^{m cosmo}
longrightarrow
mathcal T_{m SI},
]

where the stages mean:

- (chi_i): local or mode-level stability coordinate;
- (mathcal M): resolved modal/eigenstructure;
- (Chi): non-scalar aggregate or relational state;
- (mathcal A_{Chi}^{m cosmo}): Stability Arc trajectory across cosmic evolution;
- (mathcal T_{m SI}): history/inheritance map linking earlier qualified state to later qualified state.

The dark-sector application is not assumed to be a new substance. DM-side observables and DE-side observables are treated as functions to be jointly tested, with (Lambda)CDM/GR retained as the null.

## 2. Literature-grounded component definitions

### 2.1 Local stability coordinate (chi)

**Primary source:** Christensen 2026, *Exceptional-point stability boundaries from quantum dissipation to cosmological acceleration* [Chr26].

Use:
- damping-ratio organization;
- critical threshold / EP2 interpretation;
- flat-(Lambda)CDM acceleration / structure-damping identity;
- extension offset as a falsifiable coordinate.

Non-negotiable limitation inherited from [Chr26]: in the flat-(Lambda)CDM background construction, the identity is a structural reformulation of standard equations, not an independent new degree of freedom.

### 2.2 Modal / eigenstructure layer (mathcal M(a,k))

**Primary source:** Nadkarni-Ghosh & Réfrégier, *The Einstein-Boltzmann equations revisited* [Nad16].

Use:
- non-autonomous Einstein-Boltzmann system;
- eigenvalue analysis across (k) and (a);
- real-to-complex transition as onset of oscillatory behavior;
- dimensionless scale coordinate (epsilon=k/(aH));
- eigenvalue-regime mapping and stiffness structure.

Constraint: our modal layer must demonstrate added scientific information beyond simply reproducing the [Nad16] regime map.

### 2.3 Representation dimensionality / scalar-to-modal qualification

**Primary source:** Lee 2026, *Complementary Roles of Distance and Growth Probes in Testing Time-Varying Dark Energy* [Lee26b].

Use:
- Fisher eigenvalue spectrum;

- explicit demonstration that geometric probes can be effectively one-dimensional while growth can activate additional information modes.

Constraint: scalar-to-modal promotion is not novel by itself. The integration must show how information dimensionality couples to a physical stability representation and later falsification.

### 2.4 Dark-sector instability / complementary-function precedent

**Primary source:** Roy et al. 2011, *Global gravitational instability of FLRW backgrounds—interpreting the dark sectors* [Roy11].

Use:
- dimensionless phase-space coordinates;
- Jacobian-eigenvalue stability;
- precedent for DM-like collapse and DE-like acceleration appearing as different instability sectors within one broader GR-based description.

Constraint: [Roy11] uses averaged backreaction and phase-space instability, not local (chi)-modal architecture. Any claimed advance must be beyond a relabeling of its instability sectors.

### 2.5 Joint background + perturbation stability controls

**Sources:** Sharma & Sur [Sha21]; Basilakos et al. [Bas19].

Use:
- background and perturbation variables in a common dynamical-system analysis;
- growth/decay branches and critical-point spectra;
- demonstration that perturbations can alter stability conclusions obtained at background level alone.

Constraint: fixed interaction or scalar-field models are model-specific comparators, not architecture-independent evidence.

### 2.6 History / inheritance layer

**Sources:** te Vrugt, Hossenfelder & Wittkowski [Vru21]; Wiegand & Buchert [Wie10].

Use:
- explicit memory terms and history dependence from Mori-Zwanzig projection in GR [Vru21];
- multiscale structure/expansion coupling and structure-emergent effective acceleration as a history-sensitive cosmological precedent [Wie10].

Constraint: SI cannot be promoted from analogy. It must specify a measurable map whose predictive information survives conditioning on ordinary transfer/history variables.

## 3. Dark-sector identifiability controls

### 3.1 Dark degeneracy

**Sources:** Kunz [Kun07b]; von Marttens et al. [Mar19].

Established fact used as a guardrail: gravity constrains total stress-energy and can leave interacting and noninteracting dark-sector descriptions degenerate unless additional perturbative/closure information is supplied.

Operational consequence: no (Chi) state is interpreted as a unique physical DM/DE decomposition unless the degeneracy is explicitly broken by independent observables.

### 3.2 Expansion-growth degeneracy breaking

**Sources:** Pèrenon et al. [Per22]; You, Cai & Yang [You26].

Established fact used as comparator:
- expansion and growth can be reconstructed jointly;
- their consistency can test departures from standard cosmology;
- nonparametric expansion-growth inference can distinguish dynamical DE from interaction without assuming fixed functional forms.

Operational consequence: the integrated architecture must add something beyond ordinary expansion-growth reconstruction.

### 3.3 Summary sufficiency and complementarity

**Source:** Sui et al. [Sui25].

Use:
- information-theoretic testing of whether cosmological summary statistics are sufficient and complementary.

Operational consequence: every lower-order representation is challenged for information sufficiency before promotion.

### 3.4 Compression can hide new physics

**Source:** Heavens, Sellentin & Jaffe [Hea20].

Use:
- explicit warning that baseline-optimized compression can suppress or remove signatures of nonstandard physics;
- preserve agnostic directions when testing beyond-baseline structure.

Operational consequence: scalar/compressed adequacy is a testable gate, not an assumption.

### 3.5 Rejection and consistency

**Source:** Nicola, Amara & Réfrégier [Nic18].

Use:
- relative-entropy consistency testing;
- posterior-predictive model rejection.

Operational consequence: refusal is allowed and must be calibration-tested against established model-checking methods.

## 4. Five-component integration gates

### F0 — component qualification

No integration begins until each component has a defined observable representation and provenance.

Required inputs:
- (chi)-layer observables / parameters;
- modal matrix or modal summary;
- candidate (Chi) construction;
- SA trajectory definition;
- SI history/inheritance variables;
- DM-side observables;
- DE-side observables;
- native model and matched adversaries.

### F1 — local (chi) independence

Question: is (chi_i) independently informative or algebraically fixed by the native model?

Disposition:
- **QUALIFIED_LOCAL** if independently informative for the frozen target;
- **COORDINATE_ONLY** if useful as a coordinate but not independent;
- **REFUSED_LOCAL** if redundant and scientifically unnecessary.

### F2 — modal necessity

Compare scalar/local representation to modal representation using both:
- information dimensionality, including (d_{m eff})-style criteria [Lee26b];
- predictive / discriminating sufficiency [Sui25].

Modal promotion requires incremental value beyond scalar (chi).

### F3 — aggregate (Chi) necessity

A candidate (Chi) is not an average of (chi_i) by default.

It must encode a relation among qualified modal/local components whose held-out information is not contained in the marginals:

[
Delta I_{Chi}
=
I(Y;Chimid chi,mathcal M,	ext{native variables})>0.
]

Equivalent preregistered predictive metrics may replace mutual information.

### F4 — Stability Arc necessity

The SA is promoted only if trajectory/history across state space provides information beyond static component values.

Compare:
- endpoint-only model;
- local-time scalar model;
- modal state model;
- full trajectory / arc model.

### F5 — Stability Inheritance necessity

SI is promoted only if an earlier qualified state improves prediction of a later state after conditioning on standard cosmological transfer/history variables.

Core test:

[
I(Y_{t+Delta};mathcal T_{m SI}mid 	ext{standard history},chi,mathcal M,Chi)>0.
]

If ordinary transfer functions, growth history, curvature variables, or native evolution equations absorb the gain, SI is refused as redundant.

### F6 — joint DM/DE function test

The integrated state must be tested simultaneously against:
- DM-side growth/clustering/lensing/potential observables;
- DE-side expansion/acceleration observables.

The goal is not to force a unified substance. The question is whether one stability architecture improves joint organization or prediction without violating dark-degeneracy limits.

### F7 — adversarial ladder

Minimum matched adversaries:
- (Lambda)CDM / GR;
- dynamical DE;
- free neutrino mass;
- modified gravity;
- interacting dark sector;
- relevant SN/systematic variants.

A gain that disappears under one ordinary adversary narrows the claim rather than being averaged away.

### F8 — representation disposition

The integrated methodology returns one of:

- **SCALAR_ADEQUATE**
- **MODAL_REQUIRED**
- **CHI_AGGREGATE_REQUIRED**
- **ARC_REQUIRED**
- **INHERITANCE_REQUIRED**
- **FULL_ARCHITECTURE_REQUIRED**
- **REFUSED_REDUNDANT**
- **REFUSED_MODEL_IMPOSED**
- **NEED_MORE_INFO**

Higher states require every lower prerequisite to be qualified or explicitly shown unnecessary.

## 5. Tooling rule

The software does not choose scientific thresholds. Thresholds, metrics, holdouts, adversaries, and target variables are supplied from a frozen preregistration.

The software's job is to:
1. validate that required evidence exists;
2. compare lower- and higher-order representations using frozen metrics;
3. preserve failed gates;
4. attach source/comparator provenance;
5. return a disposition plus a machine-readable audit trail.

## 6. Direct-collision status

The dedicated direct-collision audit found no external paper assembling the complete five-part chain. Strong partial collisions remain:

Therefore the current novelty target is the **integration and its calibrated decision logic**, not the ingredients.

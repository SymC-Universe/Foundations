## Executive result

The current chi-architecture investigation is **not in confirmatory protocol violation** because it remains P0 exploratory work. The underlying projects already contain strong anti-retuning and layer-separation safeguards. The main pre-audit problem was that the generalized cross-regime interpretation had advanced beyond its dedicated repository record. This audit has now closed that P0 governance gap and established the first reproducible known-truth harness.

The principal conceptual correction is that scalar, vector/modal, and conglomerate/system are the current **starting representation components**, not a complete decomposition of chi. Their relationships, feedback, disagreement, inheritance, emergence, and additional structure remain objects of investigation.

The principal governance correction is narrower than initially suspected: GRI already records a candidate latent-state/chi hypothesis, explicit failure criteria, and a concrete example in which an attractive scalar/global-geometry bridge was retired after adversarial correction. What remains newly post-result is the **generalized cross-regime chi-architecture hypothesis** and the **coupling-dependent compensatory-discordance hypothesis**. Those carry promotion debt and cannot be represented as prospective confirmations.

## Positive evidence-governance finding from GRI

The current GRI reconstruction provides a concrete example of the behavior the generalized workstream should inherit:

1. GRI completed its static C1 architecture while explicitly withholding biological chi and historical GRI from the construction.
2. A post-C1 relationship between methylation spectral concentration and raw cross-cancer CKA excess initially appeared attractive.
3. An adversarial null-floor analysis showed that the relationship was largely explained by variation in the CKA permutation floor.
4. The relationship was explicitly retired as evidence for a shared scalar/global-geometry coordinate rather than rescued by changing the statistic or narrative.
5. A different scalar-to-patient-Hallmark relationship survived its corresponding floor control and was retained in narrower form.
6. The GRI supplement separately specifies conditions that must fail or narrow its future latent-state/chi hypothesis, including failure to improve held-out prediction/calibration/interpretability, instability across cohorts, elimination by subtype/composition controls, independent modal/scalar reorganization, decoupling of cross-layer geometry from state-coordinate movement, or equivalence/superiority of a simpler established model.

This is **strong compliance** with the truth-over-continuity and anti-hand-waving intent of the General Protocol. It does not confirm the generalized chi architecture; it provides a governance precedent for how that hypothesis must be treated.

## Specific scientific corrections

### 1. The triplet is a starting basis, not the answer

Invalid shorthand for program use:

`chi = scalar + modal + conglomerate`

Current protocol-compliant framing:

`{scalar, vector/modal, conglomerate/system} -> coupled structure -> emergent stability architecture -> progressively reconstructed chi`

The workstream remains open to additional components, relationship variables, or cases where one starting representation is not applicable.

### 2. Disagreement is not compensation

The possibility that disagreement may participate in compensatory whole-system behavior is a legitimate post-result discovery.

It does not yet establish feedback, compensation, homeostasis, synergy, causality, or a new chi component.

The current P0 battery now separates process noise, feedback/sensor noise, observer-only noise, absent/insufficient/stabilizing/sign-reversed feedback, and explicit nonfunctional/destabilizing/epistemic alternatives. These are known-truth method tests, not biological findings.

### 3. Algebraic conglomeration is not scientific added value

If a full operator is reconstructed algebraically from its spectrum and modes, then showing that the operator contains both is tautological.

A scientific conglomerate claim requires an independently defined property or outcome for which the coupling/whole-system object adds information, mechanism, robustness, transfer, uncertainty calibration, boundary localization, or prediction beyond the lower-level representations and the appropriate native comparator.

F0 and F6 deliberately protect this distinction: a standard method can be `EQUIVALENT`, and standard non-normal analysis is allowed to explain the effect unless the architecture adds value on a separately declared axis.

### 4. GRI is a discovery source for generalized chi architecture, not its untouched confirmation

GRI may continue to motivate and stress the generalized framework because its rebuilt architecture exposed useful successes and failures.

Any later confirmatory claim that the generalized framework applies to GRI must use a new frozen rule and decisive evidence that was not used to formulate that rule. No scalar may be selected from already-viewed GRI outcomes because it maximizes agreement with the observed architecture and then be promoted as biological chi.

The existing C1 result itself remains exactly what its frozen claim ceiling permits: static methylation/RNA architecture, not generalized chi confirmation.

### 5. Novelty review is context, not evidence of added value

The prior-art review of eigenstructure, Floquet, Oseledets/Lyapunov, resolvent, Koopman/DMD, transfer-operator, balanced-truncation, robust-control, PCA/SVD, CCA/PLS/DIABLO, and MOFA families narrows the possible novelty claim.

Until source identities and an exact comparison question are committed in a P1 comparator record, that review is literature context only. It does not satisfy MFR-05 and does not establish `ADDS`.

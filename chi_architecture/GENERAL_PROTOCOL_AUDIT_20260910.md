## Executive result

The current chi-architecture investigation is **not in confirmatory protocol violation** because it remains P0 exploratory work. However, the scientific conversation has advanced beyond the repository record and created several governance and interpretation gaps that must be closed before substantial computation is treated as evidence.

The principal correction is conceptual: scalar, vector/modal, and conglomerate/system are the current **starting representation components**, not a complete decomposition of chi. Their relationships, feedback, disagreement, inheritance, emergence, and additional structure remain objects of investigation.

The principal governance correction is epistemic: the generalized chi-architecture idea and the disagreement/compensation idea arose after viewing existing results, especially GRI. They therefore carry post-result promotion debt and cannot be represented as prospective confirmations.

## Specific scientific corrections

### 1. The triplet is a starting basis, not the answer

Invalid shorthand for program use:

`chi = scalar + modal + conglomerate`

Current protocol-compliant framing:

`{scalar, vector/modal, conglomerate/system} -> coupled structure -> emergent stability architecture -> progressively reconstructed chi`

This workstream must remain open to additional components, relationship variables, or cases where one starting representation is not applicable.

### 2. Disagreement is not compensation

The observation that disagreement may coincide with stronger conglomerate organization is a legitimate post-result discovery.

It does not yet establish:

- feedback;
- compensation;
- homeostasis;
- synergy;
- causality;
- or a new chi component.

Those interpretations require independently specified observables and controls.

At minimum, later tests must distinguish:

- epistemic/measurement disagreement;
- process-noise disagreement;
- destabilizing discordance;
- compensatory response;
- redundant information;
- synergistic joint information;
- coupling-specific effects;
- and nonidentifiability.

### 3. Algebraic conglomeration is not scientific added value

If a full operator is reconstructed algebraically from its spectrum and modes, then showing that the operator contains both is tautological.

A scientific conglomerate claim requires an independently defined property or outcome for which the coupling/whole-system object adds information, mechanism, robustness, transfer, or prediction beyond the lower-level representations under an appropriate comparator.

### 4. GRI cannot be the derivation and confirmation dataset for generalized chi architecture

GRI may remain a discovery source because its rebuilt architecture motivated the current hypothesis.

The generalized framework should first be stress-tested on known-truth synthetic systems and native mathematical examples. Any later confirmatory return to GRI must use rules frozen before the decisive GRI evidence used for that claim is opened.

No scalar may be selected from GRI because it maximizes agreement with the already observed GRI architecture and then be promoted as biological chi.

### 5. Novelty review is context, not evidence

The recent review of eigenstructure, Floquet, Oseledets/Lyapunov, resolvent, Koopman/DMD, transfer-operator, balanced-truncation, robust-control, PCA/SVD, CCA/PLS/DIABLO, and MOFA families narrows the possible novelty claim.

Until source identities and the exact frozen comparison question are committed in a comparator record, that review is literature context only. It does not satisfy MFR-05 and does not establish ADDS.

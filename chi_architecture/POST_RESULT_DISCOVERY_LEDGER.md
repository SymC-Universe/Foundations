## Historical provenance note

The current generalized architecture hypothesis has two different kinds of provenance that must not be conflated.

1. **User-reported preexisting conceptual premise, documentary verification pending:** before the later scalar mathematics, the motivating idea was that systems are shaped by pressure, influence, coupling, and feedback from other systems/components, and that coupled or grouped systems can contain scientifically important organization not visible from isolated systems alone.
2. **Post-result mathematical/generalized formulation:** the present scalar/modal/conglomerate-plus-relationships architecture was sharpened after the Stability Arc, GRI, and substrate-inheritance results and therefore carries the post-result promotion debt recorded below.

The scalar-first research phase is recorded as an investigative strategy, not as evidence that the scalar was ever assumed to be the fundamental ontology. The damping-ratio construction provided a tractable mathematical probe that was investigated aggressively and has since been shown to be insufficient as a complete description outside its licensed role.

If the original dated paper or other pre-mathematical record is recovered, it should be preserved unchanged and linked here as hypothesis-provenance evidence. It would establish chronology only, not validate the current hypothesis.

Universality is not a promotion target. The architecture may earn broader scope only through additional bounded tests. `BROAD != UNIVERSAL`.

- the three starting representations are convenient bookkeeping rather than stability-specific structure;
- each domain requires incompatible definitions with no nontrivial transportable architecture;
- any cross-domain match is produced by flexible relabeling after outcomes are known;

## CA-D002: Coupling-dependent compensatory discordance

**Selection status:** POST_RESULT_DISCOVERY  
**Hypothesis provenance:** DATA_DERIVED + FRAMEWORK_DERIVED  
**Observed trigger:** After observing that GRI retains distinct scalar, modal, and system-level descriptions rather than collapsing them, a subsequent conceptual discussion raised the possibility that disagreement between lower-level views may sometimes accompany a system-level response that preserves organization. GRI itself has not demonstrated causal feedback compensation.  
**Reason selected:** The hypothesis would give cross-view disagreement an operational role instead of treating all disagreement as measurement failure or averaging it away.

### Candidate hypothesis

Under some native feedback architectures, a perturbation can drive scalar and modal responses in different or opposing directions while the coupled system preserves or recovers an independently measured stability property. In such cases the discordance may be a signature of compensation mediated by coupling.

### Required semantic firewall

`DISAGREEMENT != COMPENSATION`

Compensation requires a prospectively specified consequence beyond the disagreement itself.

### Competing explanations

- observation/estimation noise;
- process noise with no compensatory function;
- destabilizing discordance;
- regression to the mean;
- redundancy between representations;
- generic dimensionality effects;
- confounding or common input;
- algebraic dependence created by the representation;
- a standard native feedback explanation with no additional chi-architecture value.

### Required falsification work before promotion

- distinguish process noise from post-measurement noise in known-truth systems;
- preserve lower-level components while ablating or rewiring coupling;
- define recovery/preservation from an independent future or held-out native observable;
- require compensation evidence to weaken/disappear when the responsible feedback path is removed;
- include systems where discordance predicts deterioration and systems where it predicts nothing;
- compare against standard control/state-space metrics.

### Untouched confirmation requirement

A future perturbational or longitudinal dataset/system under a frozen rule. Static cross-sectional GRI cannot by itself pay this debt.

**Current promotion status:** UNPAID_PROMOTION_DEBT / MECHANISTIC_HYPOTHESIS

## CA-D003: Noise-entry-point distinction

**Selection status:** POST_RESULT_DISCOVERY  
**Hypothesis provenance:** FRAMEWORK_DERIVED  
**Observed trigger:** Discussion of whether scalar/modal disagreement could be dismissed as noise exposed that noise entering the physical feedback loop is scientifically different from noise added only to observation after the system evolves.

### Candidate methodological proposition

Any future discordance/compensation test should distinguish at least:

1. **process disturbance:** perturbation enters the governing dynamics;
2. **feedback/sensor disturbance:** perturbation enters a measurement used by the system's controller or response mechanism;
3. **observer-only noise:** perturbation enters only the investigator's measurement/estimation path.

These are not assumed to be new chi components.

### Falsifier / narrowing condition

If the proposed classification does not change inference or predictive performance under known-truth tests, it remains an ordinary noise-model distinction and receives no special architecture status.

**Current promotion status:** EXPLORATORY_METHOD_RULE

## CA-D004: Conglomerate irreducibility requires outcome-independent added information

**Selection status:** POST_RESULT_DISCOVERY  
**Hypothesis provenance:** FRAMEWORK_DERIVED + EXTERNAL_THEORY_CONTEXT  
**Observed trigger:** Novelty and circularity review showed that spectra, modes, and operator reconstructions are established mathematics. Therefore the mere identity `operator = modal basis + spectrum` cannot establish a new conglomerate stability contribution.

### Candidate methodological proposition

A conglomerate/system representation earns a distinct scientific role only if its coupling or organization explains, predicts, localizes, transfers, or falsifies an independently defined native stability property beyond what the appropriate lower-level representations and native comparator provide.

### Failure conditions

- apparent gain follows algebraically from reconstructing the same object;
- feature concatenation alone creates the gain without a native coupling interpretation;
- a simple native comparator performs equivalently or better;
- the effect disappears after nuisance-preserving nulls;
- the claimed conglomerate cannot be defined before the target outcome is viewed.

## CA-D005: Cross-project identifiability and independence constraints

**Selection status:** POST_RESULT_METHOD_UPDATE  
**Hypothesis provenance:** DATA_DERIVED + FRAMEWORK_DERIVED + CROSS_PROJECT_AUDIT  
**Observed trigger:** Current review of GRI, Chemistry Barrier-Height/Rate Atlas, and NSD exposed recurring failure modes that can manufacture or erase apparent architecture if they are not controlled before interpretation. The detailed source audit is preserved in `CROSS_PROJECT_UPDATE_AUDIT_20260910.md`.

### Methodological proposition

A future architecture claim is admissible only to the extent that the native model is adequate, the reported structure is observable/identifiable, experimental units and null exchangeability are valid, predictor/outcome constructions do not create unexamined shared-input dependence, and every required evidence axis remains independently satisfied.

### New constraints entering future experiment design

1. **Model adequacy precedes architecture interpretation.** Estimator/model inadequacy can mimic layer disagreement or apparent system reorganization.
2. **Observable rank may be lower than latent rank.** Hidden structure may not be manufactured because a simulator or theory contains it.
3. **Identifiability propagates upward.** A conglomerate representation cannot reintroduce individual modal detail already refused as non-identifiable; use invariant subspaces/clusters/projectors or return PARTIAL/UNRESOLVED.
4. **Independent units must be declared before inference.** Technical replicates, repeated measures, ordered conditions, and independent biological/physical replicates are not interchangeable.
5. **Permutation exchangeability is structural.** Nulls must preserve the dependence structure of the actual experimental design.
6. **Shared-input algebra requires an explicit audit.** A target and predictor sharing denominators, baselines, normalizations, or sample-derived references can create association without new physical/biological information.
7. **Atlas evidence is read-only relative to Engine rules.** Reference values may not tune the System Model/Engine that is later evaluated against those same references.
8. **Evidence axes do not compensate for one another.** Provenance, mechanism identity, condition match, model adequacy, uncertainty, and comparator independence remain separately adjudicated.
9. **Network/system topology is domain licensed.** A network-resolved representation is required where native chemistry/dynamics demand it, not inserted merely because richer conglomeration is attractive.
10. **Prospective failures remain failures.** Post-hoc mechanism diagnosis can justify a new version but cannot reverse the official prospective result that motivated the diagnosis.

### Evidence-status firewall

The reviewed projects are now calibration/provenance inputs for generalized chi-architecture work. In particular:

- GRI C1/FINAL_HOLDOUT do not become confirmation of generalized chi architecture or biological feedback;
- GRI Stage D has not yet produced licensed biological recovery/compensation evidence;
- Barrier Atlas conglomerate evidence integration is not a physical chi conglomerate validation;
- NSD Phase 0C v0.2 remains official prospective `FAIL / FAIL / FAIL`; its post-hoc local-order dissociations are development evidence only.

### Consequence for current P0 plan

`P0_EXPERIMENT_PLAN_v0.2.json` is preserved unchanged because these constraints were reviewed after that plan existed. Any evaluative experiment that incorporates them must receive a new version and identify GRI, Barrier Atlas, and NSD as calibration sources.

### Untouched confirmation requirement

Future decisive evidence must be a new system/domain, or a genuinely untouched future partition/perturbation whose rules and mappings are frozen before outcome inspection and that was not used to generate these method constraints.

**Current promotion status:** EXPLORATORY_METHOD_RULE / CALIBRATION_ONLY

## Native prior art

Primary source:

Lanigan-Atkins, T. et al. *Two-dimensional overdamped fluctuations of the soft perovskite lattice in CsPbBr3*. Nature Materials 20, 977-983 (2021). DOI: 10.1038/s41563-021-00947-y.

The study uses momentum-resolved neutron and X-ray scattering with first-principles simulations and reports low-energy overdamped phonons spanning Brillouin-zone edges, interpreted as two-dimensional correlated octahedral-rotation sheets. Publisher source workbooks are supplied for the figures.

A later open-access treatment of halide-perovskite phonon dynamics makes the DHO convention explicit:

Fransson, E. et al. *Limits of the phonon quasi-particle picture at the cubic-to-tetragonal phase transition in halide perovskites*. Communications Physics 6 (2023). DOI: 10.1038/s42005-023-01297-8.

In that convention the DHO crosses from underdamped to overdamped at omega0 = Gamma/2. Therefore a candidate ratio Gamma/(2 omega0) has the same critical boundary as the licensed second-order chi coordinate, **but only if the Lanigan-Atkins source-data convention is shown to be the same or exactly convertible**.

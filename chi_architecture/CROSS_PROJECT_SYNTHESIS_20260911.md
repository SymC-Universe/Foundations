## 1. Neural Stability Dynamics: component chi, feedback closure, and recovery

Current source branch: `SymC-Universe/Biomedical:neural-stability-dynamics-phase0d-v0.2-protocol-v0.7.1`.

### P0-D14: population coordinate versus finite-sample recoverability

The exact population covariance-Hankel representation recovered the constructed second-order chi essentially to machine precision across underdamped, repeated-root, and overdamped branches. The finite-sample failure mechanism was loss of practical resolution/conditioning as the second supported direction weakened, not disappearance of the underlying mathematical coordinate.

Important separation:

`mathematically admitted coordinate != empirically identifiable coordinate in a finite record`

The current architecture must therefore retain at least these distinct states:

- coordinate physically/mathematically absent;
- coordinate admitted but non-identifiable or weakly resolved;
- coordinate admitted and adequately resolved.

A point estimate biased toward the neighborhood of chi approximately 1 under weak support cannot be interpreted as evidence that the native system is actually near that value.

### P0-D15: uncertainty belongs beside the coordinate

Branch-complete uncertainty could be propagated to `chi` and to the signed discriminant coordinate `delta_chi = chi^2 - 1`, but the current batch-Hankel calibration was not globally reliable enough to freeze a confidence or branch rule.

Program consequence:

`uncertainty is metadata on coordinate placement, not a numerical ingredient to average into Chi`

### P0-D16: fixed local chi, changing global lineages under closed feedback

Two local second-order subsystems were held fixed at chi values 0.55 and 0.85 while reciprocal feedback strength was varied. One-way influence without a return path preserved the autonomous spectrum as the union of the local spectra. Reciprocal closed-loop feedback changed the emergent global eigenstructure and global chi lineages despite unchanged local chi.

The first global lineage crossed the repeated-root boundary and reached chi about 2.30 at the upper tested coupling, while the second moved in the opposite direction toward about 0.59.

Bounded mechanistic result:

`fixed local coordinates + changed feedback closure -> changed emergent global coordinates`

This rules out arithmetic averaging of local chi as a sufficient description of the coupled mechanism in this construction.

### P0-D17: feedback geometry matters beyond coupling magnitude

With local subsystem dynamics and directed edge norms held fixed, changing only the transformation geometry of outgoing/return pathways produced materially different feedback-return operators, spectra, and global chi lineages.

Program consequence:

`coupling magnitude != conglomerate`

The conglomerate must preserve the directed transformation, the receiving/intermediary dynamics, return pathway, and frequency dependence where those are physically relevant. Basis relabeling must remain distinguishable from a physical pathway change.

### P0-D19: feedback creates a functioning region and a limit region

The P0-D19 workflow completed successfully on 2026-09-11. For the one-way architecture, the asymptotic decay rate remained essentially fixed across coupling. For the bidirectional closed loop, moderate coupling improved asymptotic recovery and stronger coupling later degraded it while the tested system remained asymptotically stable.

Representative closed-loop recovery time `tau = 1/|spectral_abscissa|`:

- g=0: about 0.0965
- g=4: about 0.0923
- g=8: about 0.0791
- g=12: about 0.0565
- g=14: about 0.0749
- g=18: about 0.1739
- g=22: about 0.5499

No recovery or chi rule was frozen.

This is a concrete P0-D example of why Function Map and Limit Map are coequal. The scientifically informative object is not merely the eventual failure boundary. The interior contains a feedback-dependent recovery optimum/turnover for this constructed system.

### NSD claim ceiling retained

These are synthetic/known-construction mechanism results. They do not establish a neural biological coupling law, a neural chi target range, or cross-domain confirmation.

## 6. Required absence taxonomy

A future statement that a system "has no chi" is too ambiguous to be scientifically useful. At minimum distinguish:

- `NO_PHYSICAL_CHI_COORDINATE`: the native governing structure does not admit the proposed coordinate under its stated assumptions;
- `CHI_COORDINATE_NONIDENTIFIABLE`: a coordinate is admitted in the model but current data/resolution cannot place it reliably;
- `MODEL_CLASS_INADEQUATE`: the system may be organized, but the current governing representation is not adequate enough to adjudicate Chi;
- `NO_ORGANIZATION_UNDER_NATIVE_CRITERION`: independently defined native organization is not established in the tested state;
- `ADMISSIBLE_MULTIDIMENSIONAL_CHI_ARCHITECTURE`: organized structure is admitted but cannot currently be reduced to one scalar without material information loss;
- `ADMISSIBLE_SCALAR_CHI`: a one-dimensional coordinate earns admission for the declared task.

No transition among these states may be inferred from preferred Atlas placement.
